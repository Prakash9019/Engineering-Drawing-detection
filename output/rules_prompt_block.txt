-- General & Engineering Notes --
  [1] Refer to ES.D.10.
  [3] Compressors are variable speed motor driven. A new motor (complete with VSD system) must be provided.
  [4] The existing ethane compressor must be modified for new process conditions defined in the process datasheet. Adequacy of all existing associated equipment (suction & discharge K.O. drums, discharge gas cooler, anti-surge control valves, auxiliaries, and other applicable items) has been verified by OEM Hitachi.
  [5] Load sharing must be provided by the ethane compressor vendor to share flow rate equally across both operating units.
  [6] The existing discharge gas cooler E-V-201, previously part of this P&ID, is now shown in new drawing no. 4224-MGDV-6-50-2004-002, along with a new discharge gas cooler.
  [7] The existing recycle anti-surge valve (including all its accessories: positioner, actuator, limit switches, position transmitter, and applicable volume tanks) must be replaced with new by Hitachi.
  [8] Existing V-RO-213 must be demolished and replaced with a new flow element (V-FE-224) by Epic Contractor. The flow transmitter (FIT) must be retained.
  [9] The arrival temperature signal will be used by the ethane compressor control system to calculate the maximum allowable arrival pressure based on the ethane fluid dew point. The calculation function block to compute the maximum pressure set point for the ethane compressor high pressure override function must be part of the ethane compressor control system. This is based on Equation 1, as defined in Operating & Control Philosophy Document No. 4224-MGDTY-6-03-2001.
  [10] Thermowell (TW), temperature element, and temperature transmitter (TT) must be replaced. Refer to the adequacy report for thermowell (4224-MGDTY-4-17-2001).
  [11] The existing suction K.O. drum internals vane pack (vendor Forain) must be replaced with new by Hitachi.
  [A] Refer to standard drawing ES.0.07.0100 REV.B.
  [NOTE:12] The Compressor Unit Control Panel (UCP) is being replaced under this project. All existing instrument connectivities, functions, interlocks, and alarms shall be configured in the newly supplied PLC-based UCP. All signals in the UCP shall also be available in the existing GDS SCADA in line with existing philosophy.
  [NOTE:13] A new safety relief valve shall be provided to safeguard the system against overpressure (blocked discharge condition). Isolation valves shall be provided with a new platform for operation.
  [NOTE:14] One common ware house spare RV shall be provided (one number) for 3 compressors.
  [NOTE:15] There is no interlocking for the isolation valve on a single RV.
  [NOTE:16] The new proposed motor enclosure (ATEX, Ex'P' type) requires instrument air.
  [NOTE:17] The existing RCI module shall be removed from the existing UCP panel and shall be re-used inside the new UCP panel.
  [NOTE:18] The Compressor Suction ESD Valve (V-ESDV-209) is retained.
  [NOTE:19] One temporary suction strainer (common for all three trains) shall be supplied by the OEM (MS. HITACHI). After successful commissioning, the strainer shall be removed and replaced with a fabricated spool piece by the EPIC contractor.
  [NOTE:20] The compressor lower design temperature is -29 DEGC, except for the dry gas seal O-ring, which is 0 DEGC. The compressor upper design temperature is 150 DEGC. However, since ASME PTC10 Type 2 test shall be conducted, the compressor casing shall be designed to 170 DEGC, which is required due to the use of CO2 as test gas. Ultimately, the name plate rating for this unit using ethane gas is 150 DEGC.
  [NOTE:21] A high performance (Class-1) non-return valve (non-slam type), with low crack open DP, shall be selected for this application. Its purpose is to avoid reverse flow / surge issues during dynamic scenarios of compressor trip.
  [NOTE:22] Existing suction and discharge compressor inlet and outlet spool shall be dismantled to facilitate compressor modification, and then re-instated.
  [NOTE:23] A soft hand switch shall be configured in the UCP HMI to enable/disable Station's high pressure controller (%), which is repeated from the load sharing panel.
  [NOTE:24] Reclosure of XV-202 (normal shutdown depressuring valve) shall be performed by UCP, once V-PAL-214 is obtained (@ 18 barg) or after 20 minutes time delay from opening of XV-202, whichever comes first. PAL-214 shall be considered an event, not an alarm.
  [SCOPE:HITACHI_EPIC_CONTRACTOR] HITACHI EPIC CONTRACTOR is part of the scope of supply.
